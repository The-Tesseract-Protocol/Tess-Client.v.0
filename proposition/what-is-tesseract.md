# What is Tesseract ?

> _“Synergy is what happens when one plus one equals ten, or a hundred, or even a thousand.”_\
> \- Stephen R. Covey

Tesseract is a **proposed institutional settlement infrastructure** being built on the Stellar network.\
Its goal is to let institutions **operate privately**, **settle publicly**, and **prove correctness mathematically,** without changing how they already work.

***

### The Core Idea

Tesseract is designed to enable institutions to:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### **Distribute payments confidentially**

Recipient lists, internal approvals, and business logic remain encrypted and institution-private.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### **Settle publicly and non-custodially**

Every payment settles on the Stellar ledger immutable, auditable, final.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### **Integrate without disruption**

Designed to connect with systems institutions already use (SAP, NetSuite, QuickBooks, Workday), not replace them.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### **Scale globally by default**

Multi-currency, cross-border settlement with \~6-second finality.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### **Reduce trust in operators over time**

Evolving from hardware-secured trust models to zero-knowledge, math-verified settlement.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

In one sentence:

#### _Tesseract is proposed settlement infrastructure that allows institutions to move money privately, globally, and verifiably at blockchain speed._

***

### Why Tesseract Exists

Institutional finance faces a structural contradiction:

* **Transparency is required** for auditability, regulators, and counterparties.
* **Confidentiality is required** for payroll, vendors, grants, and treasury strategy.

Today, institutions are forced to choose:

* Traditional banking → private, but slow, opaque, and trust-heavy
* Public blockchains → fast and auditable, but operationally exposed
* Private ledgers → confidential, but unverifiable and siloed

Tesseract is proposed to remove this trade-off.

***

### The Inflection Point

Three independent truths now converge:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### **Blockchains proved transparency works**

Public ledgers are immutable, auditable, and globally accessible.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### **Cryptography proved privacy works**

Zero-knowledge proofs make it possible to prove correctness without revealing data.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### **Stellar proved payments work at scale**

Low fees, fast finality, and a design optimised for real-world settlement.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Tesseract is designed to combine all three, **not as theory, but as infrastructure**.

***

### What Makes Tesseract Different

Tesseract is not positioned as:

* A new blockchain or rollup
* A privacy coin or mixer
* A custodial payment processor
* A fork or modification of Stellar

Instead, it is an **integration layer** that composes **existing Stellar primitives** into an institutional workflow:

* Public settlement remains public
* Confidential operations remain private
* Enforcement moves to protocol logic
* Trust shifts progressively from humans to mathematics

The system is explicitly designed to evolve:

* **MVP**: Hardware-secured trust, encrypted auditability
* **Post MVP**: Zero-knowledge proofs for trust-less verification, on-off ramps expansion, multi-currency support
* **Long term**: CBDC-grade settlement infrastructure

***
