# How to Read This GitBook

This GitBook is intentionally structured for **different readers with different goals**.\
You do **not** need to read it linearly.

Below are recommended reading paths based on _why_ you are here.

***

### 1. Product & Strategy Readers

**(Founders, operators, partners, ecosystem contributors)**

**Goal:** Understand _what Tesseract is_, _why it exists_, and _what problem it solves_ without technical depth.

**Recommended path:**

1. [**What Is Tesseract**](../proposition/what-is-tesseract.md)\
   Core product idea, positioning, and philosophy.
2. [**The Problem & Proposition**](why-do-we-need-tess/)
   * Why public blockchains fail institutions today
   * Why privacy alone is not enough
   * Why Tesseract is different
3. [**Compliance, Auditability & Institutional Controls**](../proposition/compliance-auditability-and-institutional-controls.md)
   * Why this is not a mixer
   * How auditability is preserved
   * How regulators fit into the model
4. [**Why Stellar**](../proposition/why-stellar-architectural-alignment-and-protocol-design.md)
   * Architectural alignment
   * Ecosystem leverage
   * Why this works _because_ of Stellar’s constraints

**Stop here** if your focus is strategy and market understanding.

***
